About ShowBio

The Problem

Rapid urbanization, declining green spaces, and climate challenges have increased the demand for sustainable landscaping, environmental consultancy, and horticulture solutions — yet reliable, technology-enabled sustainability services under one platform remain hard to find.

Our Solution

ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. is a sustainability consultancy providing integrated solutions in landscaping, horticulture, urban greening, plantation, environmental awareness, and AgriTech innovation — across residential, commercial, educational, and institutional clients.

Meet the Founder

Leadership

MSA

Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer

Founder & Chief Horticulturist, ShowBio Pvt. Ltd.

Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer holds a B.Sc. in Horticulture and an MBA in Marketing and Operations from IMT-Hyderabad. He is deeply rooted in agriculture and connected to a network of freelance horticulturists and gold-medalist consultants who specialize in crop selection, soil health, irrigation planning, pest control, and value-added plant products. This expert network keeps ShowBio technically updated with ground-level realities and field nuances, ensuring highly practical and localized solutions.

Shoeib brings 2.2 years of managerial experience in FMCG, having worked closely with CXOs at Reliance Fashion & Lifestyle, and has chosen to fully dedicate himself to ShowBio by turning down offers from top firms like Zomato, PhonePe, and Softcell. His experience as a sales consultant across IT, Robotics, and Education domains gives the venture a strong go-to-market and customer-centric foundation.
