Meet the Founder

Leadership

MSA

Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer

Founder, ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. · Professional Agriculture, Horticulture & Sustainability Consultant

🏆 Winner – Best AgriTech Company 2025 · Emerging Startup Award 2025 · Top 50 Startups of India

Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer is the founder of ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. and a professional agriculture, horticulture and sustainability consultant based in Hyderabad. He holds a B.Sc. in Horticulture and an MBA in Marketing and Operations from IMT-Hyderabad. He is deeply rooted in agriculture and connected to a network of freelance horticulturists and gold-medalist consultants who specialize in crop selection, soil health, irrigation planning, pest control, and value-added plant products. This expert network keeps ShowBio technically updated with ground-level realities and field nuances, ensuring highly practical and localized solutions.

Shoeib brings 2.2 years of managerial experience in FMCG, having worked closely with CXOs at Reliance Fashion & Lifestyle, and has chosen to fully dedicate himself to ShowBio by turning down offers from top firms like Zomato, PhonePe, and Softcell. His experience as a sales consultant across IT, Robotics, and Education domains gives the venture a strong go-to-market and customer-centric foundation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about ShowBio

What does ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. do?

ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. is a Hyderabad-based agriculture, horticulture and sustainability consultancy. It delivers landscaping, urban greening, plantation, green infrastructure, environmental awareness and AgriTech solutions for homes, businesses, educational institutions and government bodies.

Who is the founder of ShowBio?

ShowBio was founded in 2021 by Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer, a professional agriculture, horticulture and sustainability consultant with a B.Sc. in Horticulture and an MBA in Marketing and Operations from IMT-Hyderabad.

Where is ShowBio located and which areas does it serve?

ShowBio is headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, and works with residential, commercial, educational and institutional clients across India.

What awards has ShowBio won?

ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. was recognised as the Best AgriTech Company 2025 by the Governments of Telangana and Karnataka. Its vertical ShowBageecha.com won the Emerging Startup Award 2025, and Plantomart.com was named among the Top 50 Startups of India.

What are ShowBio's business verticals?

ShowBio operates four verticals: ShowBageecha.com for landscaping and green infrastructure, Plantomart.com as a plants and gardening marketplace, ShowBio AI & Robotics for smart agriculture technology, and ShowPlants, a social initiative for plantation drives and environmental education.

Does ShowBio offer internships and startup mentorship?

Yes. ShowBio has provided internships to 100+ students with hands-on project experience and professional mentorship, and it mentors aspiring entrepreneurs and early-stage startups with business guidance, innovation support and strategic direction.
