About the Founder

Who is Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer?

MSA

Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer is a professional agriculture, horticulture and sustainability consultant and the founder of ShowBio Pvt. Ltd., which he started in Hyderabad in 2021. He holds a B.Sc. in Horticulture and an MBA in Marketing and Operations from IMT-Hyderabad, combining field-level agronomy with business strategy.

Through ShowBio, he works with homeowners, businesses, educational institutions and government bodies on landscaping, urban greening, plantation, green infrastructure and AgriTech projects. He is connected to a network of freelance horticulturists and gold-medalist consultants specialising in crop selection, soil health, irrigation planning, pest control and value-added plant products — keeping ShowBio's advice practical, current and suited to local conditions.

Before founding ShowBio, Shoeib spent 2.2 years in FMCG management, working closely with CXOs at Reliance Fashion & Lifestyle, and worked as a sales consultant across IT, Robotics and Education. He turned down offers from firms including Zomato, PhonePe and Softcell to work on ShowBio full time.

Under his leadership, ShowBio has provided internships to 100+ students, giving them hands-on industry exposure, practical project experience and professional mentorship. ShowBio also mentors aspiring entrepreneurs and early-stage startups with business guidance, innovation support and strategic direction, helping founders turn ideas into sustainable ventures.
