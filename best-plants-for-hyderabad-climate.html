Best Plants for Hyderabad’s Climate

A horticulturist’s shortlist of trees, shrubs, ground covers, herbs and indoor plants that thrive in the Deccan heat.

By Mohammed Shoeib Ahmer, Founder of ShowBio Pvt. Ltd. · B.Sc. Horticulture · Published 12 November 2026

Understanding Hyderabad’s climate

Hyderabad sits on the Deccan Plateau and has a hot, semi-arid climate: very hot, dry summers from March to early June, a south-west monsoon from June to September, and mild, dry winters. Soils are often red and gravelly with low organic matter. Plants that do well here tolerate heat, short dry spells and well-drained soil.

Trees for gardens, campuses and avenues

  • Neem: hardy, drought-tolerant and a natural shade tree.
  • Pongamia (kanuga): a native, fast-growing shade tree suited to compounds and roadsides.
  • Indian laburnum (amaltas): spectacular yellow flowers in summer.
  • Gulmohar: red-orange summer blooms and wide shade for large spaces.
  • Moringa (drumstick): fast-growing, useful and very heat-tolerant.

Flowering shrubs

  • Bougainvillea: thrives on heat and little water; ideal for compound walls.
  • Hibiscus: flowers most of the year with regular watering.
  • Ixora: compact and colourful; prefers slightly acidic, well-fed soil.
  • Tecoma (yellow bells) and plumbago: tough, low-maintenance hedging and borders.
  • Jasmine (mogra): fragrant summer flowers for pots and borders.

Ground covers and lawn alternatives

Large lawns use a lot of water in Hyderabad’s summer. Consider replacing part of the lawn with wedelia, portulaca or ornamental grasses, which cover the ground with far less water. Where you do want a lawn, choose a warm-season grass suited to Indian conditions and water early in the morning.

Herbs and kitchen plants

  • Curry leaf, tulsi (holy basil) and lemongrass — all very well suited to local conditions.
  • Mint and coriander — grow best in partial shade and in the cooler months.
  • Green chilli and tomato — productive in pots with full sun and regular feeding.

Indoor plants that tolerate heat and low light

  • Snake plant (sansevieria) and ZZ plant — very forgiving and low-water.
  • Money plant (pothos) and philodendron — easy trailing greenery.
  • Areca palm and peace lily — need bright, indirect light and regular watering.

Planting season and watering tips

  • Best time to plant trees and shrubs: at the start of the monsoon (June–July), so roots establish before the dry season.
  • Winter vegetables and flowers: sow from October to November.
  • Summer care: water early in the morning, mulch the soil with dry leaves or wood chips, and give young plants temporary shade.
  • Soil: add plenty of compost to red soils to improve water holding and nutrients.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time to plant trees in Hyderabad?

The start of the monsoon, around June and July, is the best time to plant trees and shrubs in Hyderabad, because regular rain helps roots establish before the dry season.

Which flowering plants grow well in Hyderabad’s heat?

Bougainvillea, hibiscus, ixora, tecoma (yellow bells), plumbago, jasmine and portulaca all perform well in Hyderabad’s hot, semi-arid climate.
